# Adventurer’s Backpack

A backpack for players who leave home with a pickaxe, a stack of torches, and no promise they’ll make it back.

**Adventurer’s Backpack** adds magical backpacks built around exploration, survival, and smart inventory management. It gives you portable storage, useful upgrades, and a small amount of magical protection without turning death into a free undo button.

This is not just a chest you carry.  
It is adventuring gear.

## A Backpack with a Little Magic

Every backpack contains a few protected slots for the things you really do not want to lose: rare drops, emergency tools, valuable resources, maps, keys, or whatever matters most in your world.

The magic is powerful, but limited. The backpack can protect important items through death, but its durability weakens each time. Higher-tier backpacks are stronger and more reliable for dangerous journeys.

Choose carefully what deserves protection.

## Backpack Tiers

Backpacks progress through practical Minecraft materials.

### Iron Backpack

A sturdy first backpack for early exploration.

### Diamond Backpack

More space, more protection, and more upgrade room for serious adventures.

### Netherite Backpack

A late-game backpack built for dangerous places and long expeditions.

Each tier improves storage, protected capacity, upgrade potential, and magical durability.

***

## Upgrades

Customize your backpack with upgrades that support different playstyles.

### Lantern Hook

Carry light with you while exploring dark caves and ruins.

A simple upgrade for players who are tired of placing torches every few blocks.

Requires **LambDynamicLights**.

### Auto Pickup

Collect nearby drops directly into your backpack.

Useful for mining, farming, mob drops, and gathering resources without filling your main inventory instantly.

### Food Pouch

Keeps food ready when your hunger gets low.

Great for long trips, combat, and players who forget to eat until it is almost too late.

### Fluid Storage

Carry useful fluids inside the backpack.

Store water or lava without stuffing your inventory full of buckets.

### Restock

Keeps your hotbar supplied from backpack storage.

Perfect for builders placing hundreds of blocks, miners using stacks of torches, or players who always want backup tools and buckets ready.

### Extra Storage

Adds more room for everything you collect along the way.

Simple, useful, and exactly what every adventurer eventually needs.

### Crafting

Adds a portable crafting grid.

Make torches, planks, tools, blocks, and emergency supplies without running back to a crafting table.

### Recall Rune

Bind a home point and two waypoint anchors, then recall back to them when your journey takes you too far from safety.

The Recall Rune is made for miners, explorers, and travelers who want a way back without making teleporting feel free or overpowered.

Shift-click a Recall Rune button to bind a location.
Click a bound location to begin recalling.

A useful upgrade for long mining trips, deep cave routes, remote build sites, and returning to important travel points without removing survival risk.

## Place It Down

A backpack can also be placed in the world and used as a temporary storage block.

Set it beside a mine entrance, inside a build site, near a farm, or at a small camp while you work. Pick it back up when you are ready to move on.

***

## Survival First

Adventurer’s Backpack is balanced around survival gameplay.

It helps you carry more, organize better, and protect a few important items, but it does not remove risk completely. Death still matters. Preparation still matters. What you choose to protect still matters.

That is the core idea of the mod:

**more freedom, not free safety.**
